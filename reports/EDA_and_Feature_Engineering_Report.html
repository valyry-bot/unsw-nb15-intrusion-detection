2. Data quality assessment

The analysis reports no missing values in the training data and no exact duplicate rows in the original training dataset. Numerical variables have heterogeneous scales and distributions, including skewness and extreme values. Categorical variables require encoding before modelling.

Identifier information and attack_cat are kept outside the predictive feature set. The target variable is label.

3. Applied exploratory data analysis

The EDA examines numerical distributions, categorical traffic characteristics, correlations and differences between normal and malicious traffic. These observations support the subsequent preprocessing and feature-engineering choices.

4. Feature engineering

5. Feature selection and PCA

Feature selection: Mutual Information followed by SelectKBest retains the 30 highest-scoring features.

PCA: PCA is applied after feature selection and scaling. The workflow retains 15 principal components, corresponding to 95.18% cumulative explained variance.
